💧 Irrigation Methods

💧 Water Saving

Drip Irrigation

Water is delivered directly to plant roots through small tubes. Saves 40–60% water. Best for vegetables, fruits and sugarcane.

1Install main pipe from water source
2Lay lateral pipes along crop rows
3Place drippers every 30–60 cm
4Run 2–4 hours daily based on crop
💡 Govt subsidy: 45–90% on drip system cost. Apply at Agriculture Dept.
🌧️ Uniform Coverage

Sprinkler Irrigation

Water is sprayed over crops like artificial rain. Good for uneven land, groundnut, wheat, ragi. Saves 25–40% water.

1Set up mainline and sub-mainline pipes
2Install sprinkler heads at intervals
3Run 1–2 hours early morning or evening
💡 Best time: 6–9 AM or 5–7 PM. Avoid midday to reduce evaporation loss.
🌊 Traditional

Flood / Furrow Irrigation

Water flows along furrows between rows. Common for paddy, sugarcane. Low cost but uses more water. Best for clay-loam flat land.

1Level the field properly (1–2% slope)
2Make furrows 25–30 cm deep between rows
3Open water channel, allow controlled flow
⚠️ Avoid waterlogging — excess water causes root rot. Ensure proper drainage.
☔ Free Water

Rainwater Harvesting

Collect rainwater in farm ponds. Reduces borewell dependency. One-time investment for long-term water security.

1Dig farm pond (20×20×3 m minimum)
2Line with HDPE sheet to prevent seepage
3Connect drip system from farm pond
💡 TNAU subsidy: Up to ₹1 lakh for farm pond. Apply at Block Agriculture Office.

🌾 Harvesting Methods

🌾 Grain Crop

Paddy (Rice) Harvesting

Harvest when 80% grains turn golden yellow. Best at 20–22% moisture. Use combine harvester for large fields.

1Stop irrigation 10–15 days before harvest
2Check grain moisture (use moisture meter)
3Harvest early morning to reduce shattering
4Thresh immediately after cutting
5Dry to 14% moisture before storage
💡 Combine harvester rental: ₹2500–4000/acre. Saves 80% of harvesting cost.
🥬 Vegetables

Vegetable Harvesting

Harvest at right maturity. Too early reduces quality, too late reduces shelf life. Use sharp knife. Harvest in early morning.

🍅 Tomato
Full red, firm texture
🌿 Lady Finger
7–10 cm length, tender
🍆 Brinjal
Shiny skin, full size
🥬 Cabbage
Firm head, 1–2 kg weight
💡 Use clean sharp tools. Clean cuts prevent disease entry. Harvest in cool weather.
🥭 Fruit Crops

Fruit Crop Harvesting

Harvest at correct maturity index. Handle carefully to avoid bruising — bruised fruits rot fast in storage.

1Check skin color, firmness and size
2Use harvesting knife or clippers — don't pull
3Place gently in padded crates — no throwing
4Grade by size A, B, C — A grade fetches 40% more price
💡 Pre-cooling within 2 hours of harvest extends shelf life by 5–7 days.
🏪 Post Harvest

Post-Harvest Storage

30–40% produce lost after harvest. Proper storage can double farmer income. Use hermetic bags for grains.

🌾
Grains: Hermetic bags. 14% moisture, 6–12 months safe
🥬
Vegetables: 2–8°C cold room. 7–21 days shelf life
🥭
Fruits: 8–12°C. Grade before storage, remove damaged
💡 Hire cold van instead of truck — reduces transit loss from 15% to 3%.

🧪 Soil Health Management

🧪 Testing

Soil Testing

Test soil every 3 years. Know pH, NPK before spending on fertilizers. Costs only ₹10–25 at govt soil testing lab.

1Collect 5–6 soil samples from different spots
2Mix, dry, take 500g to soil testing lab
3Wait 7–10 days for report
4Apply fertilizers as per soil card recommendation
💡 Soil Health Card: Free testing at govt labs. Visit nearest KVK or Agriculture Office.
🌿 Organic Matter

Composting at Farm

Make compost from crop residues, cow dung, kitchen waste. Ready in 45–90 days. Almost free. Improves soil structure.

1Layer dry waste + wet waste + soil alternately
2Keep moisture like a wrung sponge
3Turn pile every 7 days for aeration
4Ready when dark, crumbly, earthy smell
💡 Add Trichoderma 500g/tonne to speed composting and suppress soil diseases.

🛡️ Pest & Disease Control

🌿 IPM Method

Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

IPM combines biological, cultural and chemical methods. Saves money and protects environment.

🐛 Step 1: Monitor
Check crop weekly for pests
🌿 Step 2: Cultural
Crop rotation, clean field
🦋 Step 3: Biological
Use Trichogramma cards, Neem oil
💊 Step 4: Chemical
Only if ETL crossed, correct dosage
⚠️ Never spray during flowering — kills bees and reduces yield by 30%.
🪤 Traps

Pest Traps & Monitoring

Yellow sticky traps for whitefly, aphids. Pheromone traps for bollworm. Light traps for night moths. All chemical-free.

1Install 10 yellow sticky traps/acre at crop height
2Count pests every 3–4 days
3Spray only if count exceeds ETL
💡 Yellow trap: ₹5–8 each, lasts 4–6 weeks. Saves ₹500–800 in pesticide costs.

🌿 Organic Farming Guide

🌱 Zero Chemical

Natural Pest Control Methods

Control pests without chemicals using neem, cow urine, garlic spray. Reduces cost by 70%.

🌿 Neem Spray
5ml neem oil + 1L water + soap. Spray weekly for sucking pests.
🧄 Garlic-Chilli Spray
100g garlic + 10 chillies + 1L water. Repels aphids, caterpillars.
🐄 Jeevamrutham
10kg cow dung + 10L cow urine + 2kg jaggery. Ferment 48 hrs. Dilute 10× and spray.
💡 Organic certified produce sells at 30–50% premium. Apply for NPOP certification.
🪱 Vermicompost

Vermicompost Production

Earthworms convert waste to compost in 45–60 days. 5× richer than ordinary compost. Sell excess at ₹8–15/kg.

1Build cement tank 6×4×2 ft or use wooden box
2Add 30% dry material as base (straw, dried leaves)
3Add pre-decomposed cow dung and organic waste
4Add Eisenia fetida worms (1kg per 100kg waste)
5Maintain 60% moisture, harvest in 45–60 days
💡 1 tonne vermicompost/year saves ₹15,000–20,000 in fertilizer cost.
